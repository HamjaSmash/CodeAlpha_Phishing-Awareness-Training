Part of the CodeAlpha Cyber Security Internship (Task 2).

### Q1. Which sender address looks suspicious?
- a) support@mybank.com
- b) support@my-bank-secure.net
- c) noreply@mybank.com

**Correct answer: b**
Attackers use look-alike domains with extra words or changed spelling. Always compare the domain with the bank's real one.

### Q2. You get an SMS: "You won 50,000 Tk! Click this link to claim." What should you do?
- a) Click quickly before the offer expires
- b) Reply with your details
- c) Ignore it, delete it and report it
- d) Forward it to your friends

**Correct answer: c**
Prize messages are a classic bait that uses greed and urgency. Clicking or replying can expose your data or install malware.

### Q3. True or False: A padlock icon in the browser means a website is safe.
- True
- False

**Correct answer: False**
The padlock only shows that the connection is encrypted. Phishing sites can also use HTTPS, so always check the domain name.

### Q4. A caller says they are from your bank and asks for the OTP sent to your phone. What do you do?
- a) Share it to verify your identity
- b) Hang up and call the bank's official number
- c) Share only half of the code

**Correct answer: b**
Banks never ask for OTPs. Anyone who does is almost certainly a scammer (vishing).

### Q5. Which of these is a typical sign of a phishing email?
- a) The link address does not match the text shown
- b) It comes from a domain you recognise and has no links
- c) It addresses you by your correct name and asks for nothing
- d) It was sent during working hours

**Correct answer: a**
Hover over a link before clicking. If the real address differs from what is displayed, it is a major red flag.

### Q6. What is spear phishing?
- a) Mass spam sent to millions of random people
- b) A targeted attack that uses personal details about the victim
- c) A virus that spreads through USB drives

**Correct answer: b**
Spear phishing is customised for one person or organisation, which makes it more convincing than ordinary spam.

### Q7. You clicked a suspicious link and entered your password. What is the first thing you should do?
- a) Do nothing and hope for the best
- b) Change the password immediately and enable 2FA
- c) Restart your computer

**Correct answer: b**
Act fast. Change the password (and anywhere you reused it), turn on 2FA, and report the incident.

### Q8. What is the safest way to open your bank's website?
- a) Click the link in an email
- b) Type the address yourself or use a saved bookmark
- c) Click the first ad in a search result

**Correct answer: b**
Typing the address or using your own bookmark avoids fake links and look-alike pages, including paid ads that imitate real sites.
